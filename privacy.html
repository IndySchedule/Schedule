Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 5, 2026

At Indy Schedule, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our website.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account data: If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile image from Google to create and manage your account.
  • Site usage and analytics: If you allow analytics, Google Analytics records page views, feature interactions, device/browser category, traffic source, and approximate location derived from IP. We do not send names, email addresses, schedules, period names, or lunch choices to Analytics.
  • App preferences: Your palette, lunch wave, period names, and display settings are saved in your browser’s localStorage. If you sign in, these preferences may also be stored in Cloud Firestore for cross-device sync.
  • Analytics preference: Your allow-or-decline choice is stored locally in your browser so the site can respect it on future visits.

2. How We Use Information

  • Provide and maintain the service, including authentication, personalization, and feature settings.
  • Operate consent-based analytics to measure visits and improve usability.
  • Sync or restore your preferences across devices when you sign in.

3. Data Storage and Security

  • Authentication and preference sync use Google Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore. Local preferences also reside in your browser’s localStorage.
  • We apply reasonable security controls but cannot guarantee absolute security on the internet.

4. Sharing Your Information

  • Service providers: Google Firebase processes optional authentication and preference syncing. If you consent, Google Analytics processes visitor usage measurements.
  • Legal: We may disclose information if required by law or to protect users and the service.
  • We do not sell or rent personal data.

5. Your Choices and Rights

  • You can sign out at any time. To request deletion of account data, email support.
  • You can clear localStorage through your browser to remove on-device preferences.
  • You can allow or disable Analytics at any time under Settings → Privacy & Terms. Declining Analytics does not affect schedule functionality.

6. Cookies and Local Storage

We use localStorage to remember site preferences and your Analytics choice. When you allow Analytics, Google Analytics may use browser storage to distinguish visits and sessions. Advertising storage, advertising user data, and ad personalization remain disabled.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted here with an updated “Last Updated” date.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact support.
