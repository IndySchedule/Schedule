Privacy at Indy Schedule

Your choices stay under your control.

This policy explains what information Indy Schedule handles, where it is stored, and the choices available to you. Most features work without an account.

01

Information We Collect

  • Account data: If you create an email/password account, Firebase Authentication processes your display name, email address, and password credential. Indy Schedule does not receive or store your plaintext password. If you use Google sign-in, Google provides your name, email address, and profile image. Firebase may also process technical information such as your IP address, browser or user agent, and security logs to operate and protect authentication.
  • Site usage and analytics: If you allow analytics, Google Analytics measures page views, feature interactions, device and browser category, traffic source, and approximate location. We do not intentionally send names, email addresses, schedules, period names, or lunch choices to Analytics.
  • Technical error reports: If you allow analytics, the site may report a short category and error code when calendar loading, account syncing, or JavaScript fails. These reports do not include error messages, page URLs, stack traces, account IDs, schedules, period names, or text you entered. A maximum of 20 sanitized records is also kept only for the current browser tab to help with troubleshooting.
  • App preferences: Your palette, lunch wave, period names, schedule choice, and display settings are saved in your browser’s localStorage. If you sign in, selected preferences are also stored in Cloud Firestore so they can sync across devices.
  • Offline files: After your first visit, your browser may keep a saved copy of the site’s code, images, and public school calendar data so the dashboard can reopen without an internet connection. This offline copy does not add your personal preferences to the public calendar data.
  • Privacy choices: Your allow-or-decline Analytics choice is stored in your browser and may be included with your synced settings when you are signed in.
  • Messages you send: If you contact support, we receive the email address and information you choose to include in your message.
02

How We Use Information

  • Show schedules and provide the features you request.
  • Create and secure optional accounts.
  • Save, sync, and restore your preferences when you sign in.
  • Measure consent-based usage trends so we can find problems and improve the site.
  • Respond to support, privacy, or deletion requests.
03

Storage, Retention, and Security

  • On your device: Local preferences remain until you clear the site’s data or your browser removes them.
  • In your account: Firebase keeps authentication records and synced preferences while your account is active. After a valid deletion request, we delete the account and its synced Indy Schedule data; service-provider backup and security records may remain for the limited periods described in Google’s policies.
  • Analytics: Analytics information is kept according to the retention settings for Indy Schedule’s Google Analytics property. Aggregated measurements may no longer identify a particular browser or visit.
  • Location and security: Google may process data in the United States or other locations where it operates. We use reasonable safeguards, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.
04

Sharing Your Information

  • Google service providers: Firebase processes authentication and preference syncing. If you consent, Google Analytics processes usage measurements. Their handling of data is also described in Firebase Privacy and Security and Google’s Privacy Policy.
  • Safety and legal requests: We may disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect a person’s safety, prevent abuse, or protect the service.
  • No selling: We do not sell or rent personal information, and we do not use it for personalized advertising.
05

Your Choices and Rights

  • You can use the schedule without creating an account, sign out at any time, or request a password-reset email.
  • To request deletion of your Firebase account and synced settings, email support, preferably from the address connected to the account. We may need to verify that the account belongs to you. Clearing browser data alone does not delete cloud data.
  • You can use the site’s local-data control or your browser settings to remove preferences stored on that device.
  • You can allow or disable Analytics at any time under Settings → Privacy & Terms. Declining Analytics does not affect schedule functionality.
  • Depending on where you live, privacy law may give you additional rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of personal information. Contact us to make a request.
06

Cookies and Local Storage

We use browser storage to remember site preferences, keep you signed in when requested, remember your Analytics choice, and save the app files needed for offline use. Your browser manages the offline cache, and you can remove it through the browser’s site-data or installed-app controls. We initialize Google Analytics measurement only after you allow it; Analytics may then use browser storage to distinguish visits and sessions. Advertising storage, advertising user data, and ad personalization remain disabled.

07

Children’s Privacy

Indy Schedule is designed for a high-school audience, and its account features are not intended for children under 13. If you are under 13, do not create an account or send personal information through the site. A parent or guardian who believes a child under 13 provided personal information can contact us to request its deletion.

08

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted here with an updated “Last Updated” date.

09

Contact Us

Indy Schedule is an independently maintained, unofficial project. If you have a privacy question or request, email indyschedule@outlook.com.
