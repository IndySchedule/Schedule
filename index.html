Indy Schedule

Indy Schedule is a fast, lightweight countdown tool built for Independence High School. It highlights the current period, displays a live countdown, and offers customizable visuals with saved user preferences.

Key Features

  • Live countdown that automatically adjusts based on the selected schedule.
  • The seven-period Independence High School regular schedule.
  • Visual customization with gradient themes, color selections, gray panel mode, and timer shadow options.
  • Optional period start/end times (Settings → Schedule) to display detailed bell information.
  • Optional Google sign-in (via Firebase) for syncing settings across devices.

Getting Started

  1. Open Settings → Appearance to customize your background, colors, or theme.
  2. Use Settings → Schedule to switch schedules or show period times.
  3. Sign in with Google if you’d like your settings to sync across devices — completely optional.

Privacy

Indy Schedule uses Firebase for optional sign-in and settings sync. With your permission, visitor usage data from Google Analytics helps improve the site. No data is sold. View the full Privacy Policy for complete details.
