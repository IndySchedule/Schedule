About

Version 1.2.0

Built for the Indy school day.

Indy Schedule is a fast, lightweight countdown and daily schedule dashboard for Independence High School.

Live countdownSee the current class and exactly how much time remains.
Calendar awareRegular, late-start, and half-day schedules switch automatically.
Made yoursChoose a palette, class names, lunch wave, and display details.
Optional syncAn Indy Schedule account can restore preferences after Chromebook storage is cleared.

Getting started

Three quick ways to make the dashboard yours.

  1. 1

    Pick your lunch wave on the Schedule page.

  2. 2

    Choose a palette and font under Appearance.

  3. 3

    Sign in only if you want settings synced between devices.

Privacy & support

Your choices stay under your control.

v1.2.0 Aug 6, 2026 Design system & final QA
  • Removed retired features, hidden controls, unused JavaScript, dead CSS, and duplicate schedule-row IDs.

  • Deploys refreshed, validated IHS calendar data directly to Firebase every three hours.
  • Separates official live events from the built-in 2026–27 bell schedule.
  • Adds explicit loading, saved-copy, offline, stale, coverage, and error states.
  • Shows calendar and lunch sources, update times, and safer official fallbacks.
  • Adds automated calendar and lunch validation before publishing.

  • Unified every Settings page with clearer cards, icons, spacing, and responsive layouts.
  • Fixed palette colors across Settings, including the selected navigation item and page icons.

v1.0.3 Aug 5, 2026 Chromebook ready
  • Optimized the dashboard and Settings workspace for 1366px school Chromebooks.
  • Accounted for the ChromeOS shelf and shorter usable browser height.
  • Changed long countdowns to an easier-to-read days, hours, and minutes format.
  • Improved schedule text contrast, progress indicators, spacing, and card proportions.

  • Added light and dark four-color palettes plus a fully custom palette editor.
  • Made cards, controls, text, and accents respond consistently to the selected palette.
  • Improved automatic foreground contrast for readable text across every theme.
  • Enabled period times by default and expanded schedule naming controls.

  • Added the Today at Indy popup with current status, dismissal, lunch, and next-day details.

  • Added calendar freshness, loading, empty, and error states.

  • Rebuilt the site for Independence High School’s seven-period day.

Support

Contact Us

Report a problem, suggest an improvement, or request a schedule correction.

Questions, corrections, and ideas are welcome.

Tell us what needs attention.

Email Indy Schedule
  • Subject — briefly name the problem, correction, or idea.
  • Page — say whether it happened on the dashboard, Today at Indy, or in Settings.
  • Details — what happened, what you expected, and what should change.
  • Screenshot — attach one when it helps explain the problem.

Quick checks

  • Wrong lunch wave? Change it under Settings → Schedule.
  • Preferences not syncing? Refresh the page, then sign out and back in.
  • Bell time or calendar event looks wrong? Report the mismatch so it can be checked against WCS information.
